### I. Fire and Bronze

*Heat drives chemistry: reduce ore with carbon, mix metals into alloys, and let steam do the work.*

1. **Pyrolysis**: 16x `minecraft:charcoal` *(after: Ready for Chemistry)*
   > Heat wood without enough oxygen to burn and the volatile compounds escape as smoke and tar.
   > What's left is charcoal: almost pure carbon. This is pyrolysis.
   > How: smelt logs in a furnace.
2. **Copper**: 16x `minecraft:copper_ingot` *(after: Ready for Chemistry)*
   > Smelting is reduction: carbon pulls oxygen away from the metal oxide and leaves metal behind.
   > How: copper ore is common near the surface, especially in dripstone caves.
3. **Tin**: 8x `modern_industrialization:raw_tin`; 8x `modern_industrialization:tin_ingot` *(after: Copper)*
   > Tin ore here comes from Modern Industrialization (MI). It's grey with pale specks and fairly common underground.
   > How: mine it and smelt the raw tin.
4. **The Forge Hammer**: `modern_industrialization:forge_hammer` *(after: Pyrolysis, Copper)*
   > MI's first workstation. It crushes ore into dust and hammers ingots into plates.
   > Ores give at most 2x here. Bigger yields come later from acids, not bigger hammers.
   > How: craft it (check JEI), place it, and right-click to use.
5. **The First Alloy**: 16x `modern_industrialization:bronze_ingot` *(after: The Forge Hammer, Tin)*
   > About 3 parts copper to 1 part tin gives bronze, which is harder than either metal alone.
   > Humanity's first engineered material, and the frame of your steam age.
   > How: crush copper and tin into dust with the Forge Hammer, mix the dusts by crafting, and smelt the bronze dust.
6. **Bronze Machine Casing**: `modern_industrialization:bronze_machine_casing` *(after: The First Alloy)*
   > The block every steam machine is built around. You'll also need one for the Dissolver in Chapter II.
   > How: bronze plates around a furnace-like core (check JEI).
7. **Steam Power**: `modern_industrialization:bronze_boiler` *(after: Bronze Machine Casing)*
   > Burn fuel, boil water, and the expanding steam does work. Welcome to the industrial revolution.
   > How: place the boiler, give it water and fuel (coal or charcoal), and place steam machines next to it. Steam flows into adjacent machines or through MI fluid pipes.
8. **Endless Water**: `modern_industrialization:bronze_water_pump` *(after: Steam Power)*
   > A steam-powered pump that pulls water from the ground, so your boiler never runs dry.
   > How: place it next to the boiler, or pipe its water into it.
9. **Steam Furnace**: `modern_industrialization:bronze_furnace` *(after: Steam Power)*
   > Smelts with steam instead of fuel. Your first automated machine.
10. **Steam Macerator**: `modern_industrialization:bronze_macerator` *(after: Steam Power)*
   > Grinds ore and items into dust: 1 ore makes 2 raw ore here (capped at 2x until acids).
11. **Steam Compressor**: `modern_industrialization:bronze_compressor` *(after: Steam Power)*
   > Presses ingots into plates. Machines need lots of plates.
12. **Steam Mixer**: `modern_industrialization:bronze_mixer` *(after: Steam Power)*
   > Mixes dusts (like copper + tin into bronze) and fluids.
13. **Steam Cutting Machine**: `modern_industrialization:bronze_cutting_machine` *(after: Steam Power)*
   > Cuts ingots into rods and planks into more planks. It uses water as coolant.
14. **Pipes**: 8x `modern_industrialization:item_pipe`; 8x `modern_industrialization:fluid_pipe` *(after: Steam Furnace, Steam Macerator, Steam Compressor, Steam Mixer, Steam Cutting Machine)*
   > Connect your machines. MI pipes carry items or fluids, including steam.
   > How: right-click a pipe end with an empty hand to set it to insert or extract.

### II. Steam and Steel

*Destructive distillation turns coal into coke, and coke turns iron into steel. At the end of this chapter, matter itself opens up to you.*

1. **The Coke Oven**: `modern_industrialization:coke_oven` *(after: Pipes)*
   > Coal is carbon mixed with many other compounds. Bake it without air and those volatiles are driven off.
   > This is destructive distillation.
   > How: the Coke Oven is a small multiblock. Look at it in JEI or use MI's guidebook to see the shape, then power it with steam.
2. **Coke**: 16x `modern_industrialization:coke` *(after: The Coke Oven)*
   > Nearly pure carbon that burns hotter than coal. It's exactly what a blast furnace needs.
3. **Byproduct: Creosote**: `modern_industrialization:creosote_bucket` *(after: The Coke Oven)*
   > The tar driven out of coal is creosote. Nothing is wasted in chemistry: creosote is a fuel for your boilers.
   > How: pipe it out of the Coke Oven into a tank, or straight into a boiler.
4. **The Steam Blast Furnace**: `modern_industrialization:steam_blast_furnace` *(after: Coke)*
   > A multiblock that gets hot enough to melt iron and dissolve carbon into it.
   > How: check the structure in JEI, then feed it steam.
5. **Steel**: 16x `modern_industrialization:steel_ingot` *(after: The Steam Blast Furnace)*
   > Iron with a small, controlled amount of carbon (about 0.2-2%) is steel: strong, tough, and the backbone of every machine to come.
   > Too much carbon and you get brittle cast iron. Chemistry is about the right ratio.
   > How: mix iron dust with coke dust, then melt it in the blast furnace (check JEI for the exact recipe).
6. **Steel Paxel**: `mekanismtools:steel_paxel` *(after: Steel)*
   > A sturdier paxel.
7. **Steel Boiler**: `modern_industrialization:steel_boiler` *(after: Steel)*
   > More steam per tick. Steel versions of the steam machines (macerator, compressor and others) run faster too.
8. **Forge Energy**: `generatorgalore:iron_generator` *(after: Steel)*
   > Some machines run on FE (Forge Energy) instead of steam. You'll need it for the Dissolver.
   > How: Generator Galore's Iron Generator burns fuel into FE. A Mekanism Heat Generator or Powah Furnator also works.
9. **The Dissolver**: `alchemistry:dissolver` *(after: Steel, Forge Energy)*
   > Your first divine gift returns.
   > The Dissolver breaks any item into the elements and compounds it's made of.
   > How: it needs steel plates and a Bronze Machine Casing. Power it with FE, insert anything, and collect the elements.
10. **The Combiner**: `alchemistry:combiner` *(after: Steel, Forge Energy)*
   > The reverse: arrange elements and the Combiner builds them back into items and blocks.
   > Taking the world apart and rebuilding it to your design is what this pack is about.
11. **First Entries in the Periodic Table**: 16x `chemlib:hydrogen`; 16x `chemlib:carbon`; 16x `chemlib:oxygen`; 16x `chemlib:iron` *(after: The Dissolver, The Combiner)*
   > Dissolve whatever you have lying around and collect your first elements:
   > H (1), C (6), O (8), Fe (26).
   > How: wood and plants give carbon, hydrogen and oxygen. Iron ingots give iron.
12. **Your First Synthesis**: 4x `chemlib:water` *(after: First Entries in the Periodic Table)*
   > Two hydrogen, one oxygen: H₂O. Build water from its elements in the Combiner.
   > It's a trivial compound, but you made it from scratch. That's the point.
   > How: the Combiner shows the recipe once you pick the target item in its GUI.

### V. Electrochemistry and Acids

*Electricity splits compounds into elements. Acids dissolve what fire can't. Ore processing becomes chemistry.*

1. **Electrolysis**: `modern_industrialization:electrolyzer` *(after: Assembler)*
   > Pass current through a compound and it splits: water into H₂ + O₂, salt water into Cl₂ and sodium.
   > This is electrochemistry, and its gases drive everything in this chapter.
2. **Salt**: 16x `modern_industrialization:salt_dust` *(after: Electrolysis)*
   > Sodium chloride, NaCl. MI salt ore is found underground; macerate it into dust.
   > Electrolysed, it gives sodium and chlorine, the start of the chlor-alkali industry.
3. **Mekanism Gases**: `mekanism:electrolytic_separator` *(after: Mekanism Circuits)*
   > Mekanism's Electrolytic Separator makes hydrogen and oxygen as gases you can pipe and store.
   > How: feed it water and FE. Use Mekanism pressurized tubes and chemical tanks for the gases.
4. **The Chemical Reactor**: `modern_industrialization:chemical_reactor` *(after: Electrolysis)*
   > MI's general-purpose reaction vessel. It combines items and fluids into new compounds. You'll use it constantly.
5. **The Contact Process, Part 1**: `mekanism:chemical_oxidizer`; `mekanism:chemical_infuser` *(after: Mekanism Gases)*
   > Burn sulfur into SO₂ in the Chemical Oxidizer, then combine it with oxygen into SO₃ in the Chemical Infuser.
   > How: sulfur comes from the Nether, or from macerating lignite coal.
6. **Sulfuric Acid**: `modern_industrialization:sulfuric_acid_bucket` *(after: The Contact Process, Part 1, The Chemical Reactor)*
   > SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄. Sulfuric acid is the most-produced chemical on Earth for a reason.
   > How: make it in Mekanism or MI and fill one bucket.
7. **3x Ore: Oxygen**: `mekanism:purification_chamber` *(after: Sulfuric Acid)*
   > Before acids, every route caps out at 2x per ore. Purifying ore with oxygen gives 3x.
8. **4x Ore: Hydrogen Chloride**: `mekanism:chemical_injection_chamber` *(after: 3x Ore: Oxygen)*
   > H₂ + Cl₂ → HCl. Injecting hydrogen chloride splits ore into shards: 4x.
   > How: combine hydrogen and chlorine in the Chemical Infuser.
9. **5x Ore: Dissolution**: `mekanism:chemical_dissolution_chamber`; `mekanism:chemical_washer`; `mekanism:chemical_crystallizer` *(after: 4x Ore: Hydrogen Chloride)*
   > Dissolve ore in sulfuric acid into a slurry, wash it, and crystallise it: 5x.
   > This is hydrometallurgy, the way most of the world's copper is really refined.
10. **MV: Aluminum**: `modern_industrialization:electronic_circuit`; `modern_industrialization:advanced_machine_hull` *(after: Electrolysis)*
   > Aluminum is the most common metal in the crust, but it's locked tightly to oxygen in bauxite. It only became cheap once electrolysis could free it.
   > How: process bauxite (MI ore) into aluminum (check JEI), then build electronic circuits and the MV hull.
11. **Uranium Dioxide**: `powah:energizing_orb`; 4x `powah:uraninite` *(after: MV: Aluminum, The Chemical Reactor)*
   > Uraninite is UO₂. Make it by oxidising uranium dust with oxygen in MI's Chemical Reactor.
   > Powah's reactors run on it, and Powah's Energizing Orb needs an MV circuit.
12. **Ore Byproducts**: `modern_industrialization:centrifuge`; 4x `modern_industrialization:silver_dust` *(after: 5x Ore: Dissolution)*
   > Real ores are never pure. Send Mekanism's dirty dust through MI's Centrifuge instead of the Enrichment Chamber and you get the same dust plus a chance at a bonus metal:
   > iron → nickel, copper → gold, gold → silver, lead → silver (galena is the world's main silver ore), tin → tungsten, osmium → iridium, uranium → lead (decay).
   > How: route dirty dust to a Centrifuge. Rare metals start arriving as a bonus.
13. **The Undergarden**: `undergarden:catalyst`; visit `undergarden:undergarden` *(after: Sulfuric Acid)*
   > A realm beneath the world. Its portal catalyst needs a bucket of sulfuric acid.
   > How: build a stone-brick portal frame and right-click it with the catalyst.

### VI. Petrochemistry

*Crude oil is a mixture. Separate it by boiling point, then build polymers from the pieces.*

1. **Black Gold**: `modern_industrialization:oil_drilling_rig`; `modern_industrialization:crude_oil_bucket` *(after: MV: Aluminum)*
   > Drill for crude oil: millions of years of compressed life, a soup of hydrocarbons.
   > How: the Oil Drilling Rig goes on an MI Quarry (check JEI).
2. **Fractional Distillation**: `modern_industrialization:distillation_tower` *(after: Black Gold)*
   > Heat the crude and each fraction boils off at its own temperature: gases at the top, then naphtha, diesel and heavy oils lower down.
   > This tower is the refinery of the pack. Every other mod gets its fuel and plastic from here.
3. **Diesel Power**: `modern_industrialization:diesel_bucket`; `modern_industrialization:lv_diesel_generator` *(after: Fractional Distillation)*
   > Diesel burns in MI generators, and later fuels your rockets (Ad Astra runs on MI diesel).
4. **Cracking**: `modern_industrialization:ethylene_bucket` *(after: Fractional Distillation)*
   > Breaking big hydrocarbon molecules into small ones gives ethylene (C₂H₄), the most important building block in plastics.
5. **Polymerisation**: `modern_industrialization:polyethylene_bucket` *(after: Cracking)*
   > Chain thousands of ethylene molecules together and you get polyethylene.
   > MI's ethylene also feeds Mekanism's HDPE plastic.
6. **HDPE**: 8x `mekanism:hdpe_pellet` *(after: Polymerisation)*
   > High-density polyethylene pellets, made in Mekanism's Pressurized Reaction Chamber.
7. **Stainless Steel**: `modern_industrialization:electric_blast_furnace`; 8x `modern_industrialization:stainless_steel_ingot` *(after: Fractional Distillation)*
   > Steel alloyed with chromium and nickel resists corrosion, so it can handle acids and hot fluids.
   > How: the Electric Blast Furnace makes it. HV parts need it.
8. **HV: Digital Circuits**: `modern_industrialization:digital_circuit`; `modern_industrialization:turbo_machine_hull` *(after: Polymerisation, Stainless Steel)*
   > Digital circuit boards are laminated with polyethylene. The HV hull follows, with a sodium battery made from your salt chemistry.
9. **The God's Archive**: `ae2:inscriber`; `ae2:controller` *(after: HV: Digital Circuits)*
   > Your memory, rebuilt as a network.
   > Applied Energistics 2 is gated by petrochemistry: the Inscriber needs a Turbo hull and the ME Controller a digital circuit.
   > How: Tom's Simple Storage was your early network. AE2 takes over from here (see Storage and Logistics).
10. **The Twilight Forest**: visit `twilightforest:twilight_forest` *(after: HV: Digital Circuits)*
   > A realm of permanent dusk.
   > How: dig a 2x2 pool of water ringed with flowers, stand in it, and throw in a diamond.

### VII. Materials and Nuclear
